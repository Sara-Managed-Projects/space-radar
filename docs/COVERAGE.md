### SUN

**The Sun**

- **Drawn by:** `site/js/scene/sun.js`, `site/js/scene/sunimage.js`
- **Default honesty:** modelled
- **On a weak device (T0, or after the frame latch):** The 2k photosphere map with modelled limb darkening; no granulation grain on tier 0 or after the latch.
- **Source:** Solar System Scope's 2k map for the disc; NOAA SWPC's sunspot regions, flares and CMEs from NASA DONKI, read live with saved copies; SDO/AIA 171 Å, the newest picture of the day (0076 W1-d).

- **Measured, modelled or reconstructed:** Measured: where the regions are, the SDO picture under Measured. Modelled: limb darkening, granulation, faculae, the corona's shape. Illustrative: the loops over the regions (Film only, labelled).
- **Rights:** Solar System Scope CC BY 4.0; NOAA and NASA data not subject to copyright in the United States; SDO courtesy of NASA/SDO and the AIA, EVE and HMI science teams.
