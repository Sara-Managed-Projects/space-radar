Sky-culture pictures in this folder (*.webp)
============================================

Originals: https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/tree/a8469197a65de582f6b0ebd82b85173145241b79/skycultures/boorong/illustrations
           Drawn by the Stellarium contributors of the Boorong sky culture (John Morieson and Alex Cherney). The sky culture's own description says, under License: "CC BY-SA 4.0".
Modified by: the Space Radar contributors, 2026-11-07. What was changed: each picture was turned to grey, put
           on black where it was transparent, resampled to a power-of-two square and re-encoded as WebP
           (scripts/build-skylore.py). Nothing was redrawn or cropped. index.json beside them repeats the
           three anchor stars Stellarium's index.json gives each picture, as sky coordinates.
