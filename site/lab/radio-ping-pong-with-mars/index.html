Radio Ping-Pong With Mars

Because radio waves travel at the speed of light, talking with a spacecraft on Mars is a slow game of ping-pong: an instruction takes 3 to 22 minutes to arrive, and its reply takes just as long to return.

What is and is not to scale.

How this was made and sources

One HTML file: no libraries, no network requests. Every number below was read on the date shown, from the page named next to it.
