What is and is not to scale.

The sheet to print

Print this page on A4 at 100 %, not "fit to page". Each disc below is the true size of its world at this scale; one wider than the paper is left out, and one smaller than 0.3 mm is drawn 0.3 mm so you can find it.

This line is 100 mm long. If your ruler says otherwise, the printer scaled the page, and every size on it is off by the same amount.

Space Radar

A free, open-source live 3D map of space: the satellites and the space station, launches, probes, planets, stars and galaxies, each drawn where it really is, with where the numbers come from.

The map needs no account. Analytics only if you say yes; no advertising, ever. MIT licence, source on GitHub.
